Commut Bus Fare Checker

Return trips from the event to drop-off hubs. One fare per seat. No service, checkout or driver fee.

Implements Rume's pricing policy as drafted. Provisional distances must be verified. Internal Commut tool.

One-way drop-off (passenger-km): the inputs

One trip after the event, from the venue to the drop-off hubs in route order. One way only. No doubled distances.

Drop-off hubs, in route order

Km is the cumulative distance from the venue along the bus's route. Type it yourself, or fetch it from the map. Typed km always works.

Optional: use "Find on map" on each hub, search the venue below, then fetch the cumulative km along the route (venue to hub 1 to hub 2 and so on).

Departure check: the inputs

  • Return only, from the event venue to the drop-off hub. No service, checkout or driver fee is added.
  • One-way drop-off: passenger-km = km × passengers, added up. Required revenue = cost ÷ (1 − margin). Common rate = required revenue ÷ passenger-km. Exact fare = km × rate, suggested fare rounded up to the next ₦100.
  • Long distance: required revenue = bus hire ÷ (1 − margin). Rate = required revenue ÷ (seats × shortest corridor km). Fare = km × rate, rounded up to the next ₦100.
  • Short distance: fare = supplier quote ÷ (passengers × (1 − margin)), rounded up to the next ₦100.
  • Shuttle trips: fare = cost ÷ (trips × seats × (1 − margin)), shown rounded up to the next ₦100.
  • Departure check: break-even bookings = cost ÷ fare, rounded up. Target-margin bookings = cost ÷ (fare × (1 − margin)), rounded up.
  • Profit = fare × bookings − supplier cost. Margin = profit ÷ revenue.
  • Distance lookup uses free public map services (OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Photon and OSRM). Please use lightly and internally only. Road distances are without traffic.
